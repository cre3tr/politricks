p9: MEMORANDUM OF OBJECTS AND REASONS
p9: Statement ofthe Objects and Reasons for the Bill
p9: The principal object of the Bill is to provide a framework for
p9: effective public participation. The Bill gives effect to the Constitutional
p9: principle of participation of the people enshrined in Articles 1, 10, 35, 69,
p9: 118, 174, 184, 196, 201, and 232 of the Constitution. The Bill also
p9: provides guiding principles to be adhered to by institutions conducting
p9: public participation. The Bill further designates the responsible authorities
p9: for purposes of the development of specific guidelines for public
p9: participation in each public institution.
p9: Part I contains preliminary provisions and includes the short title,
p9: interpretation, object of the,Act, application of the Act and principles of
p9: public participation.
p9: Part II designates the responsible authorities for the purpose of
p9: implementation of the Act.
p9: Part III provides for general guidelines for public participation and
p9: includes provisions on considerations in undertaking public participation,
p9: notice for public participation, modes of notifying the public, submissions
p9: during public participation, reasonable time, processing of responses,
p9: access to public participation processes, access to documents.and conduct
p9: in aforum ofpublic participation.
p9: Part IV provides for specific guidelines for public participation and
p9: includes provisions on development of specific guidelines for public
p9: participation, publication of the specific public participation guidelines
p9: and public participation by Parliament.
p9: Part V contains miscellaneous provisions which provide for certain
p9: information not to be disclosed, general penalty and transitional
p9: provisions.
p9: Statement on the delegation of legislative powers and limitation of
p9: fundamental rights and freedoms.
p9: The Bill delegates legislative powers to the responsible authority to
p9: make specific guidelines for public participation.
p9: The Bill does not limit any of the rights and fundamental freedoms
p9: contained in the Bill ofRights in the Constitution.
