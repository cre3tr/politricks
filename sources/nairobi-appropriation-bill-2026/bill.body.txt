p2: THE NAIROBI CITY COUNTY APPROPRIATION BILL, 2026
p2: A Bill for
p2: AN ACT of the County Assembly of Nairobi City to authorize the
p2: issue of a sum of money out of the County Revenue Fund and its
p2: application towards the service of the year ending on the 30th
p2: June, 2027 and to appropriate that sum and a sum voted on
p2: account by the Nairobi City County Assembly for certain public
p2: services and purposes
p2: ENACTED by the County Assembly of Nairobi City, as follows—
p2: Short title
p2: 1. This Act may be cited as the Nairobi City County Appropriation
p2: Act, 2026.
p2: Issue of KSh. 49,271,943,291 out of the County Revenue Fund for
p2: public services and purposes of the year ending 30th June, 2027 and
p2: appropriation of the money granted
p2: 2. (1) The County Treasury may issue out of the County Revenue
p2: Fund and apply towards the supply granted for the service of the year
p2: ending on the 30th June, 2027, the sum of Kenya Shillings Forty-Nine
p2: Billion, Two Hundred and Seventy-One Million, Nine Hundred and
p2: Forty-Three Thousand, Two Hundred and Ninety One and that sum
p2: shall be deemed to have been appropriated as from 1st July, 2026, for the
p2: services and purposes specified in the Schedule annexed hereto.
p2: (2) The sum granted out of the County Revenue Fund by subsection
p2: (3) as authorized by the Nairobi City County Assembly on the 28th July,
p2: 2026, in accordance with Article 224 of the Constitution.
p2: Appropriations in Aid
p2: 3. In addition to the sum granted by section 4, there may be applied,
p2: for the services and purposes specified in the Schedule, the sums specified
p2: out of any money directed to be applied as Appropriations in Aid under
p2: Article 206(1)(b) of the Constitution.
