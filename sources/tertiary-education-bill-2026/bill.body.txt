p7: A Bill for
p7: AN ACT of Parliament to provide for the placement of

p7: PART I—PRELIMINARY
p7: 1. This Act may be cited as the Tertiary Education
p7: Placement and Funding Act, 2026.
p7: 2. In this Act, unless the context otherwise requires–
p7: “accepting institution” means a tertiary institution that
p7: accepts a student who has been placed by the Kenya
p7: Universities and Colleges Central Placement Service in a
p7: different tertiary institution during the inter institution
p7: transfers;
p7: “admission” means the process of accepting an
p7: applicant who meets the minimum requirements set by the
p7: relevant authorities for the programme applied for in a
p7: tertiary institution;
p7: “Authority” means the Tertiary Education Funding
p7: Authority established under section 28;
p7: “Cabinet Secretary” means the Cabinet Secretary for
p7: the time being responsible for matters relating to tertiary
p7: education;
p7: “Chief Executive Officer means the Chief Executive
p7: Officer of the Kenya Universities and Colleges Central
p7: Placement Service or the Tertiary Education Funding
p7: Authority;
p7: “Commission” means the Commission for University
p7: Education established under section 4 of the Universities
p7: Act;
p7: “eligible person” means any person, who being a
p7: Kenyan citizen has attained a minimum entry requirement
p8: and has been admitted to a recognized tertiary institution or
p8: any other person being a Kenyan citizen, is willing to
p8: access tertiary education loan for the benefit of a student
p8: admitted in a tertiary institution;
p8: “emolument” means any salary, wages, remuneration,
p8: allowances, bonus, commission, gratuity, benefit or other
p8: payments or benefit whether in cash or in-kind payable to a
p8: beneficiary in respect to employment or services rendered;
p8: “enrolment” means the total number of students and
p8: trainees admitted in a tertiary institution to undertake a
p8: course or programme in a given period;
p8: “Fund” means the Tertiary Education Fund established
p8: under section 43;
p8: “loan” means financial aid granted to a student or a
p8: trainee undertaking tertiary education;
p8: “loanee” means a person who has received a loan
p8: facility for tertiary education;
p8: “placement” means allocating qualified students and
p8: trainees to specific programmes or courses in various
p8: tertiary institutions;
p8: “Placement Service” means the Kenya Universities
p8: and Colleges Central Placement Service established under
p8: section 6;
p8: “regulatory bodies” means the Commission of
p8: University Education established under section 4 of the
p8: Universities Act and the Technical and Vocational
p8: Education and Training Authority established under section
p8: 6 of the Technical and Vocational Education and Training
p8: Act;
p8: “saving scheme” means a savings plan, specifically
p8: designed by the Tertiary Education Funding Authority to
p8: enable a person to plan or invest for the tertiary education
p8: of a student’s or trainee;
p8: “scholarship” means the financial aid awarded to
p8: student or trainee, based on their need to support tertiary
p8: education;
p8: “student” means any person undertaking tertiary
p8: education in a university;
p9: “tertiary education” means formal post-secondary
p9: education offered in a tertiary institution;
p9: “tertiary institution” means a university, college, and
p9: technical and vocational education training institution
p9: recognized by the relevant regulatory bodies; and
p9: “trainee” means any person undertaking tertiary
p9: education in a college.
p9: 3. The object of this Act shall be to provide a
p9: framework for—
p9: (a) the placement of students and trainees to tertiary
p9: institutions;
p9: (b) financing students and trainees undertaking
p9: tertiary education; and
p9: (c) offering coordinated career guidance to students
p9: and trainees.
p9: 4. The implementation of this Act shall ensure—
p9: (a) universal access to tertiary education;
p9: (b) equal access to government funding;
p9: (c) availability of adequate funds through promotion
p9: of diversified funding sources;
p9: (d) sustainable source of funding; and
p9: (e) prudent utilization of funds.
p9: 5. This Act shall apply to–
p9: (a) persons seeking placement in tertiary institutions;
p9: (b) students or trainees seeking funding for tertiary
p9: education;
p9: (c) students or trainees granted funding for tertiary
p9: education;
p9: (d) Kenyan students and trainees pursuing tertiary
p9: education within and outside Kenya;
p9: (e) accredited tertiary institutions offering tertiary
p9: education in Kenya; and
p9: (f) any person or institution interested in funding in
p9: tertiary education.
