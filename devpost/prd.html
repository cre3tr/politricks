PoliTricks

A mobile-first guide to Kenyan bills: what it does, who it hits, what changed, and how to have your say, in EN, SW and Sheng, always beside the source.

Scope → PRD. Scope said "pick a bill, read what it means for you." Now we know exactly what that means: a one-time "who are you?", two ways in (Active Bills and Una'tafuta?), and three tabs per bill, with your groups first and every note beside its clause.

The core journey

Features & acceptance criteria

First-Visit Profile step 1

As a citizen, I want to say once who I am so every bill shows me what affects people like me first.

  • Groups: Employee / salaried · Small trader / hustler · Boda / matatu / driver · Student / youth. More than one can be picked.
  • Short free text in their own words ("I sell mitumba Gikomba"): length-limited, sanitised, not rewritten.
  • Their own words drive matching: clauses matching what they wrote float up alongside their groups.
  • Skip works; a skipped profile shows all groups.
  • On a return visit the screen doesn't appear and the profile is applied.
Home: Active Bills & Una'tafuta? steps 1 & 4
  • Active Bills: title, stage, and a deadline if participation is open.
  • Una'tafuta?: search recently passed bills by name or keyword; results carry a Passed label.
What It Does step 1 · the kernel

As a citizen, I want to understand a bill in my language so I can judge it myself.

  • Your groups first, each linking to the clauses behind it.
  • "See everyone" plus editable "I am…" chips for this bill.
  • Short whole-bill summary above the clause cards.
  • Every clause card shows the legal text beside its note. Nothing is explained without its source.
  • Neutral: says what it does, never what to think.
Language Switching step 1
  • A global EN / SW / Sheng toggle switches all explanations and is remembered.
  • A single card can be flipped on its own.
  • Legal source text stays as published.
Have Your Say step 2
  • Open: deadline, where to submit, and what's needed, each linked to its official notice.
  • Every deadline and stage shows a "last checked" date.
  • Not open: "Participation not open", the current stage, and what usually happens next, sourced.
  • Passed bill: becomes Where it can still be challenged; general, sourced, never legal advice.
What Changed step 3
  • Two versions: side-by-side changed clauses, each with a note.
  • One version: an honest note plus the bill compared with the current law it amends.
  • Passed: bill as introduced vs Act as passed, showing what survived and what was dropped.
Automatic Bill Intake & County steps 4 & 6
  • New national bills and Gazette notices appear without manual entry.
  • Nairobi County Assembly items appear, labelled as county. County bills use the bill page; motions use a simpler card (resolution text plus trilingual note).
Constitution Check step 5
  • Clause cards list generated article suggestions, always labelled "Suggested articles".
  • Tapping one opens the article in the app, with a link to the official source.
  • The in-app text is verifiably official: a hashed copy proves it's unaltered. The mechanism is for 4-spec.
  • Never states or implies "unconstitutional"; the reader judges.
