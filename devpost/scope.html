Scope · approved

PoliTricks

A citizen's guide to Kenyan bills. Pick one, see what it does and what changed, in English, Swahili or Sheng, and learn how and when to have your say.

The kernel

"PoliTricks": the tricks played in politics, in plain sight but buried under access, jargon and volume.

Kenyan legislation in the language people actually speak, with every explanation sitting beside the original clause, so the reader can check the AI against the source.

User → loop → proof

The "oh, that's cool" moment: a Finance Bill clause explained in Sheng, right next to the legal text.

Who & why

Who

An ordinary Kenyan citizen, not a lawyer. Today: a dense PDF or social-media hot takes, and by the time they understand the bill, participation is over.

Why it matters

Bills sit in dense, unsearchable PDFs, which makes public participation "reactive rather than proactive." This project turns that around in time to act.

Recorded disagreement: steps 4–6 go past the course's 2–4-hour target. The learner kept them, citing no hard time constraint. Build order is the control: whatever is finished gets demoed.

Stance & guardrails

Neutral explainer

Explains what a bill does and never tells people what to think. Trustworthy across camps and harder to attack.

Source beside summary

Clause text beside every explanation, and an official notice behind every date. Sheng is reviewed by the learner.
